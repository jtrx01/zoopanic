Zoo Panic – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Zoo Panic ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android, published by [PUBLISHER NAME]. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Data the game itself stores

The game does not ask for your name, email address or any account. Your best score and your sound and music settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • interactions with ads and app usage data, such as app launches and ad taps
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information

This data is processed by Google, not by us. Data is encrypted in transit. You can read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Pause → Privacy & ad settings in the game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • The game works without an internet connection. When offline, no ads are loaded and no data is sent.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
